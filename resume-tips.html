Resume Tips for your next opportunity.

Build a resume that makes your skills, projects, and experience easy for employers to understand.

01

Lead with a clear target

Put the role you want near the top so recruiters immediately understand where you fit.

02

Show results, not duties

Describe what you accomplished, improved, built, or supported instead of only listing responsibilities.

03

Keep it easy to scan

Use short sections, consistent spacing, strong headings, and relevant keywords from the job description.

Start with the essentials

Your resume should quickly answer three questions: who you are, what you can do, and why you fit this role.

What to include

  • A focused professional summary or objective
  • Education and relevant coursework
  • Technical and soft skills you can actually demonstrate
  • Projects, internships, freelance work, or organizations
  • Certifications and relevant achievements

Make projects count

For students and fresh graduates, projects can carry a lot of weight. Explain the problem, your contribution, the tools you used, and the result.

Final check

  • Match your resume to the job without inventing experience
  • Remove outdated or irrelevant information
  • Check spelling, dates, links, and contact details
  • Export a clean PDF unless the employer requests another format
