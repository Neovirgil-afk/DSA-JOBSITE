✦ INTERVIEW GUIDE

Interview Prep for your next opportunity.

Prepare with a simple structure so you can answer clearly, show confidence, and connect your experience to the role.

01

Know the role

Review the job description and identify the skills, responsibilities, and problems the employer needs solved.

02

Prepare your stories

Have short examples ready for teamwork, challenges, mistakes, achievements, and learning something new.

03

Practice out loud

Speaking your answers is different from thinking about them. Practice until your answers sound natural rather than memorized.

Before the interview

Research the company, understand the position, review your resume, and prepare a few questions to ask at the end.

Questions worth practicing

  • Tell me about yourself.
  • Why are you interested in this role?
  • What is a project you are proud of?
  • Tell me about a difficult problem you solved.
  • What skill are you currently improving?
  • Why should we hire you?

For technical interviews

Explain your reasoning before jumping to the answer. State your assumptions, approach, trade-offs, and how you would test your solution.

Interview day checklist

  • Test your camera, microphone, and internet for online interviews
  • Arrive or join the meeting a few minutes early
  • Keep your resume and job description nearby
  • Answer directly, then give a short example
  • Ask thoughtful questions about the role and team
