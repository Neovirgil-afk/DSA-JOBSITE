✦ ABOUT JOBSITE

A career workspace built to help you move forward.

JobSite connects job discovery, resume tools, skill matching, and learning so candidates and employers can work from one place.

01

For job seekers

Search opportunities, compare skill requirements, build a resume, track applications, and discover learning resources.

02

For employers

Post jobs, review applicants, compare skill matches, manage application stages, and monitor recruitment analytics.

03

Our technology

JobSite connects resume analysis, skill-gap detection, job matching, career paths, and data-structure algorithms into one workflow.

How JobSite works

Users build their profile and resume, then our system detects skills and compares them with job requirements. Matching results help candidates discover relevant jobs and identify skills they can learn next.
