Your information should support your career, not get in the way.

Information we use

JobSite may store account information, profile details, skills, resumes, applications, saved jobs, and learning progress so the platform can provide its features.

Resume privacy

Uploaded resumes are handled through authenticated application and employer flows. Access should remain limited to the user and authorized employer workflows.

Your responsibility
